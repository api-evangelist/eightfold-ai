Eightfold AI is an agentic Talent Intelligence Platform that combines enterprise HR data with insights from more than a billion career profiles to power talent acquisition, talent management, workforce planning, internal mobility, resource management, and AI-driven interviewing. The platform is delivered as enterprise SaaS with sales-led onboarding; APIs and ATS/HRIS integrations are exposed to customers and certified partners (e.g. Oracle Fusion Cloud Recruiting) under contract rather than via a public, self-serve developer portal.

**Areas:** Talent Intelligence · Talent Acquisition · Talent Management · AI Interviewing · Human Resources · Workforce Planning · AI Agents

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Eightfold AI: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
